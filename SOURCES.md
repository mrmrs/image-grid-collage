# Image sources

37 locally bundled Unsplash photographs. Original photo pages, photographer credits, download URLs, and dimensions are recorded in `public/image-library.json`. All images remain local at runtime.

The original 25 images were verified on October 14, 2026. Twelve additions were verified on October 26, 2026: eight monochrome portraits, one color portrait, two studies of hands/figures, and one architectural study. Each new photo page explicitly identifies the photographer and the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). No premium Unsplash+ images are included.

Original downloads are up to 2400 pixels wide; additions are capped at 2400 pixels on the longest edge, JPEG quality 90, sRGB, with their original aspect ratio and photographic tones. New library thumbnails are capped at 320 pixels. `node scripts/download-images.mjs` restores both originals and thumbnails from the manifest. Higher-resolution originals are available on the linked photo pages.

The social card uses a generated composition of Jalil Saeidi's portrait (first entry), with the same credit and license as the source photograph. It is generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`.
